# 太醫院日記 壹號（韓國藏書閣 K2-5077 冊001） / Taeuiwon ilgi [Royal Medical Office Diary], Volume 1: Transcription Dataset

《太醫院日記》壹號是韓國學中央研究院藏書閣 K2-5077 冊001，自隆熙二年（1908）五月起記錄宮廷值宿。日記事項包括入診時刻、症候與後續照護、齒科診療、假齒製作、看護及宮廷巡行的醫療安排。原件以漢文為主，另有韓字夾注和陰、陽曆並記。

This first volume of the Korean Royal Medical Office Diary opens in 1908 and is held by Jangseogak as K2-5077, volume 001. It records physicians’ duty shifts, examinations and follow-up care, dental treatment and prostheses, nursing and arrangements during court travel. Most entries are in Literary Chinese and some contain Korean glosses and mixed-script notes.

依冊001的55幅定位，分存封面、正文欄、夾注、韓字與可辨印記。跨頁照護事件及曆日保持原欄界。

The dataset indexes all 55 images of volume 001. Covers, body columns, marginal notes, Korean text and visible marks are separate. Dated entries follow the source column breaks.

## 文字與覆核 / Text and review

逐幅回圖，比對獨立讀稿及原初錄，採用 42 項回圖訂正。疑讀、藏印或細小批注按實際可辨範圍保留。未做人類全文雙校、專家審定、同儕審查或準確率量測。日期以影像所載文字為準。淡灰背印、界欄與黑墨正文分層。部分小字、印文和韓字留疑。

Source-image review compared independent observations with the initial transcription and records 42 image-supported changes. Uncertain readings remain marked. Human full-text double proofreading, expert review, peer review and accuracy measurement were not performed. Dates are recorded as written. Show-through, rulings and body writing are distinguished. Some small notes, seal readings and Korean text remain uncertain.

TXT 依幅、面與欄提供閱讀定位。JSON 和 TEI 採同一固定取文。「□」標難辨字，缺幅或不確定缺字處由頁面說明及疑義資料標示。完整提示、私有覆核過程與專用程式未隨附。AI 實際模型路由與用量未知。
